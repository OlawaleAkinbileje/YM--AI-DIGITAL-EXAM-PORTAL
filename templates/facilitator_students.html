📋 How to share in 3 clicks in Google Sheets:
  1. Open your spreadsheet in Google Sheets and click the blue Share button at the top right.
  2. Under General access, change Restricted to Anyone with the link (set to Viewer).
  3. Click Copy link, paste it into the box above, and click Connect & Sync Sheet below.
